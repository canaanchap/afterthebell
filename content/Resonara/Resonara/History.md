Below is only a short and abbreviated and myopic timeline of events.

---

Year 1 AR · [[Primen]] 1
[[The First Ringing]]
The [[Great Celestial Bell|celestial bell]] sounds for the first time, and the world of [[Resonara]] springs into existence. The [[Ten Ancestral Voices]] answer the call, each claiming a [[month]] in the new [[calendar]].

Year 147 AR · [[Verdane]] 31
[[The Elven Concordance]]
The elven clans of [[Graymantle Forest]] establish their sacred covenant, planting the [[Great Oak of Whispers]] that would stand for millennia. The [[Sunken Hollow]] is consecrated as their eternal resting place.

Year 203 AR · [[Meylis]] 17
[[First Sighting of Ignathar]]
A young red [[dragon]] claims the volcanic caves of the [[Ashfall Peaks]] as his domain, beginning a reign of fire that continues to the present day.

Year 341 AR · [[Harkan]] 8
[[Forging of Tidecaller]]
The legendary trident [[Tidecaller]] is forged during a more rare double [[moon]] alignment in the coastal forges, imbued with the power to command the seas. Later lost in the [[Saltmere Marshes]].

Year 347 AR · [[Verdane]] 21
[[The Starfall Crater]]
A [[fragment of the celestial void]] tears through the sky and crashes into the [[northern wastes]], creating what will later be known as the foundation of [[Blackspire]]. Reality grows thin at the impact site.

Year 401 AR · [[Dirgesong]] 12
[[The Sunken Crown Unearthed]]
A ceremonial crown of unknown metal is discovered in a flooded burial chamber. It cannot be melted, broken, or cleaned, and it always feels cold to the touch.

Year 428 AR · [[Harkan]] 16
[[The Starfall]]
A [[celestial being]] crashes into the structure that will become [[Blackspire]]. The impact leaves a permanent scar in the world, and the tower begins whispering to those who approach.

Year 463 AR · [[Seravene]] 33
[[The Bellroot Grove Discovered]]
Explorers find a [[Bellroot Grove|grove of pale trees]] whose roots coil around ancient stone bells buried in the earth. When touched, the bells hum softly as if remembering the [[First Ringing]].

Year 499 AR · [[Meylis]] 28
[[The First Moonbinding]]
A [[COASTAL ORDER MYSTICS TBD|coastal order of mystics]] successfully binds a [[fragment of moonlight]] into a crystal vial, creating the first known “[[lunar relic]].” The method is lost within a century.

Year 536 AR · [[Verdane]] 14
[[The Glass Wastes Form]]
A lightning storm unlike any before strikes the [[central plains]] for thirteen consecutive days, fusing sand into jagged glass fields that reflect moonlight in unsettling patterns.

Year 612 AR · [[Meylis]] 7
[[Aelric, the First Restless Dead]]
A shepherd named [[Aelric]] becomes the first recorded death on [[Resonara]], succumbing to fever in a remote village; his spirit cannot find rest and becomes bound to the world. Later [[scholars]] dispute definitions of "first," distinguishing this case from the first death formally recorded by organized [[archivists]].

Year 667 AR · [[Greymark]] 2
[[The Sable Choir Sings]]
A [[Sable Choir|band of robed singers]] appears at dusk in a ruined amphitheater, performing a wordless song that causes listeners to weep uncontrollably. They vanish by morning, leaving [[black feathers]] behind.

Year 702 AR · [[Primen]] 19
[[The Thornwood First Stirs]]
Travelers report a [[Thornwood|forest]] that “moves when no one watches.” Trees lean into paths, roots rise like fingers, and compasses spin wildly near its edge.

Year 751 AR · [[Avenyl]] 25
[[The Ember Covenant Formed]]
A small [[FIRE-KEEPER SECT TBD|sect of fire-keepers]] swears to protect the [[Ashfall Peaks]]’ sacred flame-shrines. Their rites later evolve into the [[cult]] that builds the [[Forge of Sorrow]].

Year 803 AR · [[Rhovan]] 3
[[The Silent Pilgrimage]]
For forty nights, [[pilgrims]] walk in total silence from the western coast to the [[Bellroot Grove]], claiming the bells “answered” their grief. None can later recall what they saw there.

Year 864 AR · [[Meridane]] 17
[[The Saltmere Lights Appear]]
Strange lights begin hovering over the [[Saltmere Marshes]] during fog. Sailors follow them and disappear; locals call them “[[Lanterns of the Drowned]].”

Year 913 AR · [[Verdane]] 40
[[The Warden Takes Oath]]
A lone guardian swears an oath to protect [[Graymantle Forest]] from any who would plunder its sacred places. Legends later name him the [[Gray Warden]].

Year 978 AR · [[Harkan]] 29
[[The First Skycarving]]
An unknown artisan carves a constellation map into a cliff face so large it can be read from miles away. The constellations do not match any known sky.

Year 1024 AR · [[Harkan]] 6
[[Veris the Scholar, First Recorded Death]]
A [[scholar]] named [[Veris the Scholar|Veris]] disputes the claim of the first death recorded in surviving [[annals]]. His burial site becomes the seed of what later myth calls the [[Endless Crypt]]. Some traditions still name [[Aelric]] centuries earlier as the first [[restless dead]]; this entry reflects the earliest surviving formal record.

Year 1073 AR · [[Dirgesong]] 9
[[The Endless Crypt Opens]]
A subterranean necropolis, the [[Endless Crypt]], is discovered beneath collapsed ruins. Each deeper chamber contains fresher corpses than the last, as if time flows backward underground.

Year 1156 AR · [[Avenyl]] 6
[[Birth of Ignathar]]
A clutch of crimson-scaled [[dragon]] eggs hatches deep within the [[Ashfall Peaks]]. The strongest [[wyrmling]] devours its siblings. [[Ignathar]]’s legend begins in blood and flame.

Year 1211 AR · [[Greymark]] 23
[[The Dreadmoor First Spreads]]
A sickly fog begins creeping across the eastern lowlands. Crops rot overnight, and insects breed in impossible numbers; the [[Dreadmoor]] swamp’s edge becomes a boundary of fear.

Year 1294 AR · [[Rhovan]] 19
[[The Bell of Echoes Cast]]
A grand bell is forged from [[meteoric ore]] and [[river-silver]]. When the [[Bell of Echoes]] is rung, it seems to repeat itself a heartbeat later, an echo that makes some listeners feel watched.

Year 1372 AR · [[Seravene]] 11
[[The First Harmony Night Recorded]]
Astronomers note a rare [[Moons of Resonara|lunar arrangement]] that causes stones, water, and even air to resonate faintly as if the world hums in remembrance. The phenomenon later becomes known as [[Harmony Night]].

Year 1451 AR · [[Meridane]] 4
[[The Widow's Creek Vanishes]]
A river known for its clear waters disappears overnight. In its place remains only damp stone and an unnatural hush. Spiderwebs begin appearing where water once flowed down; men who visit often never return, giving name to the [[Widow's Creek]].

Year 1533 AR · [[Primen]] 26
[[The Blackspire Builders Arrive]]
A nameless [[MASON GROUP TBD|order of masons]] appears at the [[Starfall]] site, claiming they were “called by the crack in the sky.” They begin building the [[Blackspire]] upward without explaining their purpose.

Year 1622 AR · [[Verdane]] 9
[[The Graymantle Schism]]
Elven clans divide over whether to hide their sacred sites from outsiders or invite allies. The dispute scars the forest’s politics for generations.

Year 1710 AR · [[Harkan]] 14
[[The Drowned Oath]]
A fleet attempts to conquer the marsh-coast and disappears in a single night. Survivors claim the sea “spoke” and demanded an oath, then dragged oathbreakers under.

Year 1769 AR · [[Greymark]] 41
[[The First Storm Crown Legend]]
Rumors spread of a crown hidden within [[Blackspire]] that commands lightning and wind. Many die attempting to climb the tower’s upper reaches.

Year 1834 AR · [[Dirgesong]] 37
[[Bjorn Frostbeard Finds the Crystal]]
Dwarf miner [[Bjorn Frostbeard|Bjorn]] discovers the cursed ice crystal in the deepest [[Frostpeak Mines|Frostpeak mine]]. Against all warnings, he touches it. Within hours, his fate is sealed, possessed by the ancient frost magic of [[Frostpeak]].

Year 1856 AR · [[Rhovan]] 31
[[Blackspire Completed]]
The [[Blackspire|tower]] reaches its final height, piercing the clouds; reality grows unstable around it, and those who climb too high report hearing whispers from beyond.

Year 1891 AR · [[Verdane]] 7
[[The Bell of Echoing Silence]]
A [[The Bell of Echoing Silence|mystical bell]] is cast that, when rung, creates a zone of absolute silence for one [[hour]]. Used in ancient rituals, it was hidden away when its power to silence even thoughts became known. The location? Lost to time.

Year 1891 AR · [[Greymark]] 6
[[The Elven Exodus]]
For reasons never fully explained, the entire [[elven]] population of [[Graymantle Forest]] vanishes overnight, leaving behind empty cities and sealed burial grounds.

Year 1923 AR · [[Meylis]] 48
[[Vex Begins to Fall]]
A celebrated [[paladin]] named [[Vex, the Paladin|Vex]] reaches the top of [[Blackspire]] seeking the [[Fallen Star]]. Instead of redemption, he finds corruption. His transformation into a dark champion begins. Later accounts place his final enthronement and full corruption in a separate, later [[era]]; this entry marks the beginning of that descent.

Year 1923 AR · [[Seravene]] 18
[[Discovery of the Widow's Lair]]
Explorers find an ancient spider's nest beneath the waterfalls of [[Widow's Creek]]; the creature within, known as [[The Widow]], has lived in the [[Widow's Lair]] for centuries and guards a hoard of [[spidersilk treasure]]s.

Year 1967 AR · [[Meylis]] 39
[[The Forge of Sorrow Founded]]
[[FIRE CULT TBD|Fire cultists]] establish a temple deep in the [[Ashfall Peaks]], believing the volcanic heat is a gift from their god; in the [[Forge of Sorrow]], they begin rituals to summon an [[elder fire elemental]].

Year 1967 AR · [[Rhovan]] 11
[[Morvaine's Madness Complete]]
The exiled ranger [[Morvaine]], after centuries of isolation and forbidden magic, completes his transformation into a corrupted beast-master. He claims the ruined temple in Graymantle as his domain.

Year 2001 AR · [[Greymark]] 29
[[The Phylactery of Endless Night]]
An obsidian vessel capable of storing a [[lich]]'s soul is crafted by unknown hands. It is said to grant true [[immortality]] but at the cost of one's humanity. Later sought by [[Mortis the Deathless]].

Year 2016 AR · [[Seravene]] 15
[[The Bale]]
At 11:30:16, an encroaching darkness sweeps the world. All consciousness ceases for twenty-eight months. When people awaken, their memories are erased, some skills and abilities remain, but most identities are lost.

Year 2017 AR (1 AB) · [[Primen]] 14
[[The Awakening of the Lost]]
The world's population awakens from [[The Bale.]] Chaos reigns as people with no memories attempt to understand their surroundings, relationships, and purposes.

Year 2018 AR (2 AB) · [[Harkan]] 9
[[First Encounter with the Undead]]
Survivors stumbling through the eastern marshlands discover [[undead|animated corpses]] wandering aimlessly; without memory of death or [[necromancy]], they flee in terror.

Year 2018 AR (2 AB) · [[Harkan]] 22
[[First Rift Anomaly]]
A massive chasm tears open in the [[eastern wasteland]], descending into impossible depths. Those who investigate report madness-inducing visions. [[The Maw ]]begins calling to the curious and the damned. This event is preserved as an early, localized opening; later centuries would see deeper fractures and eventual quarantine-era exploration.

Year 2020 AR (4 AB) · [[Primen]] 7
[[The Library Founded]]
[[Keeper Aldric the Rememberer]] and seven other knowledge-seekers establish what will become the [[Great Library]], initially dismissed as "[[The Cult of the Forgotten]]."

Year 2021 AR (5 AB) · [[Rhovan]] 26
[[Rediscovery of Mystical Fire]]
A survivor relearns the principles of creating fire through mental effort and [[willpower]] alone; the technique spreads rapidly, transforming post-[[Bale]] survival prospects.

Year 2023 AR (7 AB) · [[Dirgesong]] 14
[[First Winter Crisis]]
Unprepared for Dirgesong's cold without collective memory of seasons, hundreds die in the [[northern settlements]]; survivors begin recording weather patterns.

Year 2024 AR (8 AB) · [[Verdane]] 38
[[Rediscovery of Ignathar's Lair]]
Post-[[The Bale|Bale]] explorers stumble upon the dragon's volcanic home in the [[Ashfall Peaks]]. [[Ignathar]], having also lost his memories, is more aggressive and territorial than ever, attacking all who approach.

Year 2025 AR (9 AB) · [[Verdane]] 17
[[The Gorman Raids Begin]]
A charismatic bandit calling himself [[Gorman the Red]] establishes a stronghold in an abandoned watchtower in the Weathered Hills and begins raiding trade routes.

Year 2028 AR (12 AB) · [[Greymark]] 33
[[Death of Aldric the Rememberer]]
The [[Keefirst Head Librarian|first Head Librarian]] dies of plague while still cataloging on his deathbed; succeeded by young [[Thessaly Moonscribe]].

Year 2031 AR (15 AB) · Avenyl 42
[[Discovery of the Crimson Waste]]
Explorers find a vast desert battlefield littered with ancient bones and rusted weapons; they report seeing [[skeletal warrior]]s walking at night but dismiss it as heat-induced visions of the [[Crimson Waste]].

Year 2031 AR (15 AB) · [[Dirgesong]] 19
[[The Frostpeak Massacre]]
The possessed [[dwarf]] [[Bjorn Frostbeard]], memory-less but still bound to the [[ice crystal]], attacks his former [[UNNAMED DWARVEN VILLAGE|mining village]]. All forty-seven residents are found frozen solid, expressions of terror etched on their faces.

Year 2034 AR (18 AB) · [[Meridane]] 11
[[Captain Dregg's First Raid]]
A [[Captain Dregg|pirate captain]] emerges in the [[Saltmere Marshes]], establishing a hidden fortress and preying on coastal settlements; his true origins remain unknown.

Year 2038 AR (22 AB) · [[Rhovan]] 18
[[Thessaly's Crisis of Faith]]
[[Thessaly Moonscribe|Keeper Thessaly]] discovers her own pre-[[The Bale|Bale]] journal describing a husband and children she cannot remember, leading to a profound personal crisis.

Year 2039 AR (23 AB) · [[Verdane]] 12
[[Thessaly Resigns]]
Unable to reconcile her lost memories with her role, [[Thessaly Moonscribe]] resigns as [[Head Librarian]] and becomes a hermit-[[astronomer]]; [[Veran the Elder]] assumes leadership.

Year 2047 AR (31 AB) · [[Meylis]] 23
[[Captain Dregg Claims His Fortress]]
The [[Captain Dregg|pirate captain]], not remembering his past life but retaining his sailing skills, seizes a [[UNNAMED AREA IN MARSH|cave complex]] in the [[Saltmere Marshes]] as his base of operations. His legend begins anew.

Year 2051 AR (35 AB) · [[Meylis]] 41
[[The Great Fire]]
A devastating fire destroys a quarter of the [[Great Library|Library]]'s recovered texts, sparking public outrage and forcing the Library to negotiate its transformation into a public institution.

Year 2053 AR (37 AB) · [[Harkan]] 22
[[The "Bibliarch" Proposed]]
[[Veran the Elder]] proposes a formal title, the "[[Bibliarch]];" to legitimize the [[Library]]'s leadership position and strengthen its political authority.

Year 2054 AR (38 AB) · [[Dirgesong]] 8
[[Assassination of Veran the Elder]]
[[Veran the Elder|Veran]] is murdered by three [[Council of the Great Library|council]] members ([[Darius the Learned|Darius]], [[Hela of the North|Hela]],[[ ]]and [[Tornin Fescoif|Tornin]]) in the [[Keepers' Revolt]]; over opposition to public access; his young son witnesses the killing.

Year 2054 AR (38 AB) · [[Dirgesong]] 15
[[Lysander Truequill Appointed]]
In emergency consensus, merchant-diplomat [[Lysander Truequill|Lysander]] becomes the first person to formally bear the title "[[Bibliarch]]," chosen as a neutral party to unite factions.

Year 2055 AR (39 AB) · [[Primen]] 21
[[The Bibliarch's Compact]]
The Library's first [[Library Creed|constitution]] is codified, defining the [[Bibliarch]]'s powers, succession process, and institutional mission.

Year 2057 AR (41 AB) · [[Avenyl]] 36
[[Thessaly Moonscribe Dies]]
The former [[Head Librarian]], living as a hermit-astronomer, passes away; she continued sending [[|astronomical observations]] to the [[Library]] until her death.

Year 2064 AR (48 AB) · [[Avenyl]] 16
[[The Weathered Hills Uprising]]
A man calling himself [[Gorman the Red]] (not remembering his original name) leads desperate survivors in the hills to turn to banditry. He claims an old watch tower as his base, preying on travelers to the mountains.

Year 2068 AR (52 AB) · [[Rhovan]] 29
[[Lysander Dies; Aleesia Appointed]]
The First [[Bibliarch]] dies peacefully at age 66; [[Aleesia First-of-Pages]] becomes Second Bibliarch at age 28, now the youngest appointed librarian.

Year 2089 AR (73 AB) · [[Seravene]] 7
[[Aleesia's Famous Address]]
At the 70th anniversary of the [[Great Library|Library]]'s founding, [[Aleesia First-of-Pages|Bibliarch Aleesia]] declares: "We are not the keepers of truth, merely its caretakers." A notion that later proves dangerous.

Year 2089 AR (73 AB) · [[Rhovan]] 31
[[Discovery of the Blade of Seasons]]
A legendary sword that changes properties with the seasons is unearthed from pre-[[Bale]] ruins. In spring it heals, summer it burns, fall it withers, winter it freezes. The Library claims the [[Blade of Seasons]] for study.

Year 2092 AR (76 AB) · [[Greymark]] 14
[[Pre-Bale Recovery Department Disbanded]]
[[Aleesia First-of-Pages|Aleesia]]'s [[Pre-Bale Recovery Department|experimental department]] to actively search ruins is quietly shut down after consuming vast resources with minimal returns.

Year 2095 AR (79 AB) · [[Meylis]] 19
[[Aleesia Dies; Veran II Appointed]]
The Second Bibliarch collapses while shelving books at age 55; [[Veran II|Veran, Second of the Name]], becomes Third Bibliarch at age 47.

Year 2111 AR (95 AB) · [[Verdane]] 32
[[School of Living Memory Founded]]
[[Veran II|Bibliarch Veran II]] establishes a training center for oral historians, formalizing the collection and preservation of spoken testimonies by trained erudites from the [[School of Living Memory]].

Year 2112 AR (96 AB) · [[Seravene]] 19
[[The Singing Stones Phenomenon]]
[[Singing Stones|Seven standing stones]] in the [[Weathered Hills]] begin emitting harmonic tones during [[Harmony Night]]s. Scholars theorize they are remnants of pre-[[Bale]] [[magic]] attempting to remember their purpose.

Year 2125 AR (109 AB) · [[Harkan]] 8
[[Kael Brightmind Dies]]
[[Veran II]]'s [[Kael Brightmind|most promising apprentice]] is killed in a riding accident, disrupting succession plans and leaving the aging Bibliarch without an heir.

Year 2130 AR (114 AB) · [[Dirgesong]] 41
[[Veran II Dies; Theron Appointed]]
The [[Veran II|Third Bibliarch]] dies peacefully at age 82; in an unexpected turn, [[Chief Architect]] [[Theron Blackbell]] becomes Fourth [[Bibliarch]].

Year 2134 AR (118 AB) · [[Greymark]] 44
[[The Gray Warden's Vigil Continues]]
Sightings confirm that the [[Gray Warden]] of [[Graymantle Forest]] survived [[The Bale]], though he remembers nothing of his purpose. He continues protecting the woods by instinct alone.

Year 2156 AR (140 AB) · [[Verdane]] 27
[[The Crimson Waste Evolves]]
Twenty years before the [[War of Red Sands]], an ancient battlefield in the [[southern arid regions]] begins exhibiting strange properties. Bones rise from the sand during [[Harmony Night]]s, dancing in formation.

Year 2159 AR (143 AB) · [[Avenyl]] 23
[[Theron's Fatal Fall; Seraphine Appointed]]
[[Theron Blackbell|Bibliarch Theron]] falls from scaffolding inspecting construction; his Second, [[Carbon]], disappears; [[Seraphine the Bright]] becomes Fifth [[Bibliarch]] at age 31.

Year 2172 AR (156 AB) · [[Primen]] 14
[[Discovery of the Vault of Echoes]]
An intact, pre-[[Bale]] Athenaeum containing 12,000 volumes is discovered beneath ancient ruins, the greatest single recovery of lost knowledge.

Year 2178 AR (162 AB) · [[Seravene]] 28
[[War of Red Sands Begins]]
Two kingdoms dispute territory in the arid southern regions; Library-backed peacekeeping forces led by [[General Karthus Ironheart]] deploy to mediate what becomes the [[War of the Red Sands]]. The five-year conflict will reshape the region forever.

Year 2183 AR (167 AB) · [[Meridane]] 35
[[Fall of General Karthus]]
The [[War of Red Sands]] ends catastrophically as [[General Karthus]] falls in what is the final battle in the desert of the [[Crimson Waste]], cursed ground where [[Karthus, Undead|Karthus]] will later rise as an undead general.

Year 2190 AR (174 AB) · [[Dirgesong]] 18
[[Death of Lyssa Starweaver]]
[[Seraphine the Bright|Seraphine]]'s favored [[Lyssa Starweaver|apprentice]] and most promising successor dies of consumption at age 29; the [[Bibliarch]] never fully recovers from the loss.

Year 2197 AR (181 AB) · [[Verdane]] 9
[[Valence Publishes Agricultural Thesis]]
Young scholar [[Valence Mortis|Valence]] of the Eastern Reaches publishes promising research on crop rotation and soil management, showing early brilliance.

Year 2200 AR (184 AB) · [[Rhovan]] 27
[[Valence Leaves the Library]]
After disagreement with [[Seraphine the Bright|Seraphine]] over research funding priorities, the agricultural scholar departs; his whereabouts become unknown.

Year 2217 AR (201 AB) · [[Greymark]] 47
[[Death of Her Knowledgable; Matthias Appointed]]
[[Seraphine the Bright|Seraphine]] dies peacefully at age 89 after 58 years, the longest [[Bibliarch]] reign to date; the realm mourns for a month; merchant [[Matthias Goldleaf]] becomes Sixth Bibliarch.

Year 2233 AR (217 AB) · [[Meylis]] 33
[[Matthias's Breaking Point]]
After harsh public comparison to [[Seraphine the Bright]], Bibliarch [[Matthias Goldleaf|Matthias]] is found drunk in the archives with an unknown woman; what is considered a scandal is quietly covered up.

Year 2235 AR (219 AB) · [[Avenyl]] 11
[[Matthias Resigns; Olivin Becomes Interim]]
Still unable to escape [[Seraphine the Bright|Seraphine]]'s shadow, and with mounting pressure relating to character and integrity, [[Matthias Goldleaf|Matthias]] voluntarily steps down; [[Treasurer-General]] [[Olivin the Steady|Olivin]] assumes interim leadership during succession crisis.

Year 2237 AR (221 AB) · [[Primen]] 28
[[Isadora Stoneheart Appointed]]
After two years of political gridlock, Scholar [[Isadora Stoneheart|Isadora]] wins appointment as Eighth Bibliarch, promising "knowledge wielded without hesitation."

Year 2250 AR (234 AB) · [[Harkan]] 16
[[Matthias Goldleaf Dies]]
The former [[Bibliarch]], living quietly as economic advisor, dies peacefully at age 92, having found peace after resignation.

Year 2255 AR (239 AB) · [[Dirgesong]] 3
[[The Purge of the Forty-Seven]]
[[Bibliarch]] [[Isadora Stoneheart|Isadora]] expels 47 scholars for "seditious teachings," including researcher [[Valence Mortis]] for "proto-necromantic" life extension research; he flees to the [[eastern marshlands]].

Year 2261 AR (245 AB) · [[Seravene]] 12
[[Marcus Stoneheart Flees]]
[[Isadora Stoneheart|Isadora]]'s nephew and groomed successor secretly joins the [[Liberalist]] opposition and abandons the [[Great Library|Library]], devastating the [[Bibliarch]].

Year 2264 AR (248 AB) · [[Greymark]] 25
[[Assassination of Isadora; Corvus Appointed]]
The Eighth [[Bibliarch]] is poisoned, and the assassin never caught; [[Liberalist]] leader [[Corvus Stoneheart|Corvus]] becomes Ninth Bibliarch in massive reform wave.

Year 2269 AR (253 AB) · Greymark 36
[[Lord Commander Vex's Fall]]
A former paladin of the [[Great Library|Library]] climbs [[Blackspire]] to purge its darkness and claim the [[Fallen Star]]'s secret. He is not slain, he is changed. Corruption crowns him, and he claims the summit as his throne. Chroniclers debate whether this is [[Vex, the Paladin|Vex]]'s final fall or a later culmination of the corruption first noted in earlier centuries.

Year 2272 AR (256 AB) · [[Verdane]] 19
[[Helena Fairvoice Leaves Library]]
[[Corvus]]'s [[Helena Fairvoice|apprentice]], attacked by all factions for attempting mediation, abandons scholarship entirely and becomes a physician.

Year 2275 AR (259 AB) · [[Rhovan]] 8
[[Corvus Dies; Tiberius Appointed]]
The Ninth [[Bibliarch]] dies of pneumonia and exhaustion at age 55; military commander [[Tiberius Flameheart]] becomes Tenth Bibliarch, promising "strength."

Year 2278 AR (262 AB) · [[Avenyl]] 34
[[The Scholar Uprising]]
[[Librarian]]s literally barricade [[Bibliarch]] [[Tiberius Flameheart|Tiberius]] out of the archives in protest; an institution-wide vote forces his abdication, becoming the first Bibliarch removed by force.

Year 2278 AR (262 AB) · [[Seravene]] 2
[[Helena Peaceweaver Returns]]
The physician formerly known as [[|Helena Fairvoice]] returns to become Eleventh [[Bibliarch]], taking a new name: "Helena Fairvoice died. [[Helena Peaceweaver|I am reborn]]."

Year 2281 AR (265 AB) · [[Primen]] 15
[[The Accords of 265]]
The constitutional framework balancing [[Bibliarch]] authority with council oversight is drafted, establishing the [[Three Pillars Doctrine]].

Year 2282 AR (266 AB) · [[Dirgesong]] 37
[[Helena Dies; Thalia Becomes Interim]]
Bibliarch [[Helena Peaceweaver|Helena]] succumbs to consumption at age 43; her Second, [[Thalia Evenstar]], assumes interim leadership to complete the [[Three Pillars Doctrine|Accords]].

Year 2283 AR (267 AB) · [[Meylis]] 44
[[Olivin the Steady Dies]]
The former interim [[Bibliarch]] passes away at the extraordinary age of 109, honored as "The Hand That Steadied the [[Bell]]."

Year 2284 AR (268 AB) · [[Verdane]] 21
[[Thalia Steps Down; Elena Appointed]]
With the [[Accords]] implemented, Interim [[Bibliarch]] [[Thalia Evenstar|Thalia]] voluntarily relinquishes power; [[Elena of the Common Field]] becomes Thirteenth Bibliarch.

Year 2290 AR (274 AB) · [[Harkan]] 6
[[Thornwood Expeditions Begin]]
[[Bibliarch]] [[Elena of the Common Field|Elena]] commissions systematic research into the spreading corruption in the interior forests, discovering it advances three feet per year.

Year 2294 AR (278 AB) · [[Greymark]] 12
[[Tiberius Dies in Exile]]
The disgraced [[Tiberius Flameheart|Tenth]] [[Bibliarch]] dies alone and forgotten in a remote mountain monastery at age 71.

Year 2310 AR (294 AB) · [[Avenyl]] 29
[[Thalia Evenstar Dies]]
The former interim [[Bibliarch]], who completed [[Helena Fairvoice|Helena Peaceweaver]]'s work, passes away at age 75 after years of teaching.

Year 2313 AR (297 AB) · [[Seravene]] 18
[[Elena Dies; Hadrian Appointed]]
Beloved [[Bibliarch]] [[Elena of the Common Field|Elena]] dies peacefully at age 71; the entire capital mourns; constitutional scholar [[Hadrian Justwords]] becomes Fourteenth Bibliarch.

Year 2321 AR (305 AB) · [[Primen]] 24
[[Threat Assessment Bureau Established]]
Bibliarch [[Hadrian Justwords|Hadrian]] creates systematic monitoring of dangerous locations with the establishment of the [[Threat Assessment Bureau]] including The [[Dreadmoor]], [[Castle Ebonwatch]], The [[Thornwood]], and The [[Crimson Waste]].

Year 2323 AR (307 AB) · [[Verdane]] 11
[[The Mortis Files Revealed]]
Intelligence confirms the lich in The [[Dreadmoor]] is the expelled scholar [[Valence Mortis]]; report notes: "We found his original thesis. It was on agricultural policy." Reactions... interesting. Is that all there is to it?

Year 2334 AR (318 AB) · [[Rhovan]] 33
[[Hadrian Dies; Octavian Appointed]]
The Fourteenth [[Bibliarch]] dies at age 77; ship captain and explorer [[Octavian Farseeker]] becomes Fifteenth Bibliarch at age 48.

Year 2343 AR (327 AB) · [[Meridane]] 9
[[The Pirate Problem Begins]]
Coastal raiders, particularly [[Captain Dregg]] in the [[Saltmere Marshes]], begin disrupting [[Great Library|Library]] maritime trade and knowledge distribution.

Year 2346 AR (330 AB) · [[Greymark]] 9
[[The Forgotten King's Seals Crack]]
Deep beneath the [[Crimson Waste]], [[Tomb of the Forgotten King|ancient tomb]] doors crack open for the first time in centuries. A pulse of necrotic energy radiates outward, and [[Seven Phylacteries|seven hidden phylacteries]] begin calling him back. This is treated as the first clear sign of return, an omen rather than a full awakening.

Year 2350 AR (334 AB) · [[Harkan]] 27
[[Failed Negotiation with Captain Dregg]]
Bibliarch [[Octavian Farseeker|Octavian]] personally offers amnesty to pirate [[Captain Dregg]]; Dregg refuses: "Gold doesn't remember who owned it last."

Year 2368 AR (352 AB) · [[Greymark]] 41
[[Octavian Lost at Sea; Valentine Appointed]]
[[Octavian Farseeker|The Navigator]] disappears during his final expedition at age 82; his body is never recovered; philosopher [[Valentine Greymantle]] becomes Sixteenth [[Bibliarch]].

Year 2377 AR (361 AB) · [[Meylis]] 15
[[The Malkor Incident]]
[[Bibliarch]] [[Valentine Greymantle|Valentine]] prevents [[High Priest Malkor]]'s attempted summoning of an [[elder fire elemental]] at the [[Forge of Sorrow]] through diplomatic intervention.

Year 2403 AR (387 AB) · [[Dirgesong]] 22
[[Valentine Dies; Dorian Nightingale Appointed]]
