## [[Major Eras of the Great Library|Major Eras]]

Only in retrospect does the [[Great Library]] note six major 

- [[Founding Era|The Founding Era]]: Takes places in the immediate aftermath of [[The Bale]]. Among the chaos, certain individuals gather in what is seen as obsessive cultists fixated on recovering lost knowledge.
- [[Consolidation Era|The Consolidation Era]]: [[The Library]] evolves from a religious cult to cultural and knowledge center as the world rebuilds.
- [[Golden Age|The Golden Age]]: A steady period of progress following the establishment of the [[Great Library]], now firmly cemented as the center of thought and culture in Resonara.
- [[Schism Era|The Schism Era]]: Rapid turnover, short tenures, and crises mark this season, after reformers surge in influence and leaders with non-traditional backgrounds–though polarizing–hold clout.
- [[Expansion Era|The Expansion Era]]: Peace again reigns as a series of strong leaders and gentle spirits lead to significant growth across all Resonara's five [[continent]]s.
- [[Modern Period|The Modern Period]]: Four and a half centuries after The Bale, the Great Library sees results and progress as it continues to share what's been learned over the years.
